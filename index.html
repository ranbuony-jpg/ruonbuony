A little about me

Learning by building

3rd Year Computer Science Student at Gambella University

My journey began with curiosity. Growing up, I was fascinated by technology and always wondered how it could solve the problems we encounter in our daily lives. I saw challenges that could have been easier with the right technology, opportunities limited by a lack of innovation, and problems that inspired me to think beyond what already existed.

That is where my passion began. I chose Computer Science because I believe technology is more than computers and programming. It is about finding better ways to do things, solving real-world problems, creating opportunities, and building a better future.

I am still learning, still exploring, and still growing, but one thing remains certain: I want my knowledge to become a solution to someone's problem.
